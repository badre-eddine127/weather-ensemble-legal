App: Weather Ensemble

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enable daily weather notifications?

Go to Settings and turn on "Daily weather notification." You'll receive today's temperature and min/max every morning at 8 AM for your current location. This feature is included with Smart Forecast premium.

How do I add a favorite city?

Tap "+ Add" under Favorite Cities in Settings, or use the location picker to search for and save a city. Your favorites sync across your devices via your account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your App Store or Google Play account settings, not within the app. Open your device's subscription settings, find Weather Ensemble, and cancel from there.
